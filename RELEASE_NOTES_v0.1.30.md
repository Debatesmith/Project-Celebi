# Project Celebi v0.1.30 — Public Beta

Project Celebi turns Pokémon Gold into an expansion-style continuation of an existing Gen 1 Gen1Recomp save.

## What carries forward

- Trainer identity
- Party and PC Pokémon
- Pokémon EXP / DVs / Stat EXP / moves / OT data
- Pokédex history
- Play time
- Money and coins
- Compatible inventory and PC items
- Kanto badges / completed Gym history
- Historical Kanto travel / Fly access
- Gen 1 HM authorization where appropriate

## Johto veteran balance

- Johto trainer levels scale around the team strength brought across the border.
- Silver, Gym Leaders, and the League receive additional difficulty bonuses.
- Ordinary Johto wild Pokémon are pulled to roughly 10–15 levels below the Project Celebi team rating.
- Kanto remains unscaled.

## Progression work

- Project Celebi Champions can walk from Route 22 through Victory Road Gate toward Johto.
- The Route 22 gate guard is removed without disturbing the separate Mt. Silver guard.
- Johto badges remain genuine progression — Project Celebi does not fake them.
- Silver's rookie Cherrygrove battle is skipped, while his later native encounters are restored to the campaign.

## Important beta disclaimer

**Dialogue and immersion changes are intentionally minimal in this release.**

Most NPC and story text is still vanilla Pokémon Gold. The gameplay systems understand that your trainer has a Gen 1 history, but the narrative frequently does not yet acknowledge it. Expect occasional dialogue that treats your imported Champion like a new trainer.

This public beta is focused on continuity, progression, balance, and bug discovery. A dedicated narrative/immersion pass can come later.

## Please report bugs with

- Project Celebi version
- Gen1Recomp version
- Source game
- Screenshot
- Affected Gold save after the issue
- `lua-error.log`, if present

**Back up your saves before testing beta builds.**
